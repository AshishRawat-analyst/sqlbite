A practical approach to learning SQL

SQLBite is built around a simple idea: learners make progress by understanding a concept, seeing it applied, and then writing queries themselves.

As a Data Analyst, I work with SQL as a tool for exploring and interpreting data. I created SQLBite to make that learning process more approachable through clear lessons, sample datasets, revision notes, and an interactive SQL practice lab.

The goal is not just to memorise syntax. It is to develop the confidence to read a data problem, choose an approach, and explain the result.
